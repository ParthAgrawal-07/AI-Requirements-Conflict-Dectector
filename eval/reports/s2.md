## 2. Candidate recall — the ceiling for every pair category

A labelled pair that never becomes a candidate can never be classified, so this is the upper bound
on recall for Conflicting, Duplicate and Dependent.

| Similarity threshold | Candidate pairs (LLM calls) | Recall, all 20 | Conflicting (8) | Duplicate (6) | Dependent (6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0.75** (current project default) | 30 | **50%** | 2 | 6 | 2 |
| 0.70 | 42 | 70% | 4 | 6 | 4 |
| 0.65 | 65 | 80% | 5 | 6 | 5 |
| **0.60** | 91 | **85%** | 6 | 6 | 5 |
| 0.55 | 167 | 85% | 6 | 6 | 5 |
| 0.50 | 264 | 90% | 7 | 6 | 5 |

Held-out split (6 issue pairs) at 0.50: 6 of 6.

**Missed at 0.75:** REQ-002–REQ-012, REQ-004–REQ-032, REQ-020–REQ-032, REQ-024–REQ-028,
REQ-036–REQ-037, REQ-038–REQ-039 (Conflicting); REQ-006–REQ-008, REQ-008–REQ-027,
REQ-040–REQ-050, REQ-046–REQ-047 (Dependent).
**Still missed at 0.50:** REQ-002–REQ-012 (encryption at rest vs. plaintext preferences — the
textbook "dissimilar-sounding conflict") and REQ-008–REQ-027 (Dependent).

### Findings

1. **The 0.75 default halves recall.** Six of the eight planted conflicts (75%) never reach the
   classifier at 0.75; four of those six score between 0.55 and 0.75. Duplicates are found at every
   threshold tried (they share wording by definition).
2. **0.60 is the knee of the curve:** 85% recall for 91 pairs, three times fewer calls than 0.50 for
   five points less recall. One full analysis of this SRS at 0.60 costs about 91 pair calls + 60
   single checks ≈ 150 LLM calls (more with re-asks), well inside the 2,000-call monthly budget.
3. **Some conflicts are not similar at all** (REQ-002–REQ-012). No threshold catches them cheaply;
   these are exactly what the rule-based cross-checks of US-58 (Sprint 5) are for.

### Recommendation (for Heet and Keval to decide)

Lower the project default `similarity_threshold` from **0.75 to 0.60** (a one-line change in the
project model and a data migration for projects still on the old default), and record the decision
in the decision log. Not changed in this sprint's code: it is a tuning decision with a cost impact,
and the classification run in §3 should confirm it first.

## 4. Reproduce

From the repository root, with the backend's dependencies installed:

```bash
# §2 — free, no API key
python eval/run_benchmark.py --embedder minilm --threshold 0.75 --json eval/reports/s2_candidates.json

# §3 — real run; records every reply so it can be re-scored without spending budget
GEMINI_API_KEY=... python eval/run_benchmark.py --embedder minilm --threshold 0.60 \
    --llm live --record eval/reports/s2_replies.json --json eval/reports/s2_run.json

# re-score a recorded run (e.g. after changing the labels)
python eval/run_benchmark.py --embedder minilm --threshold 0.60 \
    --llm replay:eval/reports/s2_replies.json
```

`--split dev` / `--split heldout` restrict the labels. Tune on `dev` only; report `heldout` at CP1.
