## Dependent

One requirement relies on the other to be satisfiable, without restating it: if the
depended-upon requirement were removed or violated, the other could not be satisfied.

- Test: could one requirement still be satisfied if the other were deleted? If not, it is
  Dependent. Set `dependency_direction` to `A_depends_on_B` or `B_depends_on_A`.
- A mere cross-reference ("as specified in REQ-012") is not a dependency by itself.
- Requirements where one simply triggers or complements the other ("log failed logins" and
  "alert after five failed logins") are None.
