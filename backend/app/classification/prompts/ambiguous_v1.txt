## Ambiguous

The wording allows more than one reasonable interpretation of what must be built or verified,
because of a specific vague word or phrase.

- Point at the word: "The system shall respond promptly" ("promptly" has no measurable bound).
- List every vague term in `vague_terms`, quoted exactly as written in the requirement.
- The message may list wording that matched the project's lint patterns (`<pattern_matches>`).
  A match is a hint, not a verdict: "fast" in "fast-track approval" names a process, not a speed.
  Decide from the whole sentence.
- Domain terms defined in the project glossary are not vague.
