## Incomplete

Every word is clear, but the requirement omits a fact a developer or tester needs, so it cannot
be implemented or verified as written.

- Example: "The system shall maintain 99.9% uptime" — the measurement window is missing.
- Typical missing facts: the actor, the trigger, a limit or unit, the error behaviour, the data
  affected. List each one in `missing_information`, as a short noun phrase a person can answer
  ("the measurement period for the 99.9% figure").
- Do not ask for implementation detail the requirement is right to leave open (technology,
  design). Missing *rationale* is not incompleteness.
- If a vague word causes the problem, the category is Ambiguous instead (mention the missing fact
  in the rationale).
